# Patisserie & Luxury Cake UI Skill

Specialized design intelligence and component architecture for bespoke cake studios, artisanal bakeries, and patisserie e-commerce (like **Meenah Cakes**).

## 1. Visual Identity & Color Psychology

Food and celebration websites must evoke **warmth, appetite, and trust** while maintaining editorial elegance:

- **Canvas / Surfaces**: Avoid stark clinical `#FFFFFF` as the primary canvas. Layer warm alabaster (`#FCF9F5`), whipped cream (`#FAF6F0`), and soft blush parchment (`#F9EFEF`) so cake photography feels naturally lit.
- **Deep Espresso / Cocoa Ink (`#2C1614` – `#3A1F1D`)**: Never use pure `#000000`. Rich roasted cocoa text pairs harmoniously with buttercream, chocolate, berries, and gold leaf.
- **Signature Raspberry / Berry Crème (`#D81B50` / `#B0123F`)**: Use high-chroma berry tones purposefully for primary conversion actions, interactive active states, and micro-accents.
- **Champagne Gold / Warm Caramel (`#C89D54` / `#E6D5B8`)**: Subtle metallic hairline borders, star ratings, and bespoke badges elevate the perceived value of wedding and celebration tiers.

## 2. Typography & Editorial Hierarchy

- **Display / Headlines**: High-contrast editorial serif (`Playfair Display` or `Fraunces`). Use tight tracking (`-0.02em`), graceful italic emphasis for emotional hooks, and balanced line breaks (`text-balance`).
- **UI & Body Copy**: Clean geometric sans-serif (`Manrope`) with generous line-height (`1.65`) for effortless scannability of flavors, notice periods, and delivery zones.
- **Micro-Labels & Badges**: Refined letter-spacing (`0.14em`–`0.18em`) paired with tactile pill containers or hairline rules.

## 3. Header & Navigation Architecture

A high-converting bakery header balances **brand romance** with **immediate ordering utility**:

1. **Top Concierge / Announcement Bar**:
   - Communicates crucial logistics upfront: *Location (Ikorodu, Lagos)*, *Lead time (48h notice / Same-day bento cakes)*, *100% Pure Butter*, and direct Instagram/WhatsApp links.
2. **Floating or Glassmorphic Main Header**:
   - Warm translucent surface (`bg-[#FCF9F5]/85 backdrop-blur-xl`) that transitions smoothly on scroll.
   - **Logo**: Artisanal emblem + refined wordmark + subtle location/tagline sub-label.
   - **Center Navigation**: Clear links (`Home`, `Our Cakes`, `About`, `Contact`) with tactile pill/underline active indicators.
   - **Right Utility Cluster**:
     - Live status pill or quick contact (*"Baking Today"* / Instagram link)
     - Interactive Shopping Bag with item badge
     - High-contrast Concierge CTA (*"Order on WhatsApp"* / *"Order Now"*) with WhatsApp icon.

## 4. Hero Section Anatomy (The "Artisanal Showcase" Pattern)

The hero must make the visitor **crave the cake within 2 seconds** and know **exactly how to order within 5 seconds**:

1. **Left Column — Story, Occasion Discovery & Conversion**:
   - **Trust / Location Eyebrow**: E.g., *"Handcrafted in Ikorodu, Lagos · 100% Pure Butter"*.
   - **Editorial Headline**: Emotional, occasion-driven promise with bespoke typographic contrast.
   - **Interactive Occasion / Category Pills**: Quick-order chips (*Wedding Tiers*, *Birthday Cakes*, *Berry Naked Cakes*, *Custom Box*) that let users preview or jump straight to their celebration type.
   - **Dual CTA Hierarchy**:
     - Primary: Direct WhatsApp / Custom Cake Order CTA with subtle glow and tactile press state.
     - Secondary: *"Explore Cake Menu"* with price anchor or gallery preview.
   - **Social Proof & Logistics Strip**: Verified customer rating (`4.9★ from 500+ celebrations`), avatar stack or cake slice thumbnails, and key guarantees (*48hr Custom Turnaround*, *Lagos-wide Delivery*).

2. **Right Column — Multi-Layered Visual Stage**:
   - **Interactive / Layered Editorial Frame**: Instead of a lone static rectangle, pair a stately arched main showcase with interactive cake switcher thumbnails or floating glassmorphic cards:
     - **Floating Tasting Note / Featured Creation Card**: Shows the name, flavor profile (e.g., *Vanilla Bean & Fresh Strawberry Compote*), and starting price or quick-order link of the currently displayed cake.
     - **Floating Craftsmanship / Review Badge**: E.g., *"100% Organic & Freshly Baked Daily"* or *"500+ Celebrations Crafted"*.
     - **Secondary Detail Thumbnail**: A tactile overlapping detail shot (like a close-up slice with raspberry filling and macarons) to prove interior crumb texture and quality.
